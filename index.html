The Story

Abhi Prakash · Los Angeles, 2026

Disciplined.
Ambitious.
Long-term.

Abhi Prakash is an operator, investor, educator, and builder working at the intersection of sports, technology, finance, and leadership. Currently pursuing an MBA at the UCLA Anderson School of Management, he is focused on building organizations and communities that create lasting impact across the West Coast and beyond.

He earned a Bachelor of Arts in Economics and a Master of Science in Information Systems from the University of Arizona, where he developed the interdisciplinary foundation that continues to shape his work across business, technology, athletics, and leadership.

His background spans corporate finance, product management, venture investing, consulting, real estate, and grassroots athletics. Across roles with Ernst & Young, General Motors, Adobe, Cardone Ventures, Asearis Data Systems, and Unrivaled Sports, he has developed a reputation for connecting strategic vision with disciplined execution.

Beyond business, Abhi is deeply committed to education, mentorship, and leadership development. Through his work with Peak Basketball and youth athletics, he believes sports can serve as a powerful vehicle for confidence, resilience, accountability, and opportunity. His leadership philosophy centers on service, long-term thinking, and helping others realize a bigger vision for themselves and their future.
